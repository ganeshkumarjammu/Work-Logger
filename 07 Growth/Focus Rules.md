# Focus Rules

1. **One Thing first.** The day's most important task gets the first uninterrupted block.
2. **Max 3 in "Now".** A fourth task waits in "Next".
3. **Timebox.** 50 minutes on, 10 off. Press Menu → S before you begin — the running clock is your commitment.
4. **Capture, don't switch.** Interruption → Menu → I, one line → back to work.
5. **Stuck for 30 minutes?** Write what you tried in the note, then ask. Asking with notes is fast; asking with nothing wastes two people's time.
6. **Problems get a next step.** Write the problem and one thing you can do about it.
7. **Shutdown every day.** Inbox cleared (Menu → P), status sent (Menu → U), tomorrow's One Thing chosen.
