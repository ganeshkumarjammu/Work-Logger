# Inbox

The one place for anything that lands on you while you are busy: a request, a thought, "check this later". You do not decide now. You decide once a day.

- **Menu → I** — type one line, Enter. It is saved under Items and your timer keeps running.
- **Menu → P** — clear the Inbox: pick an item, then pick what it becomes (do it now, task for later, small to-do, project info, or delete).
- Any new note you create without choosing a place also lands in this `00 Inbox` folder, and is counted until you move it.
