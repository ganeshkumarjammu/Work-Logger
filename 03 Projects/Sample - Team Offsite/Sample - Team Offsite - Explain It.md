# Sample - Team Offsite - Explain It

Fill this as the project goes and finish it at the end. If you can say it aloud without reading, you understand the project.

## One line
- I organised a one-day planning offsite for 12 people within budget.

## 30 seconds (problem → what I did → result)
- The team needed one uninterrupted day to agree the quarter plan. I fixed the date first, booked a venue 20 minutes away, and sent the agenda a week ahead. (Result to be added when the day has happened.)

## 2 minutes
1. The problem and why it mattered: planning was happening in scattered one-hour meetings and never finished.
2. What I did (3 steps): fixed the date with the team lead; booked the venue; sent invitation and agenda.
3. Why this way and not another: a nearby venue costs half and loses no travel time.
4. Result (with a number): venue booked at 10% discount; (attendance and outcome to be added).
5. Hardest part and how I solved it: two venues were not free; I now ask about the date first.
6. What I would do differently: confirm travel plans of the team before choosing the date.

## Questions I may be asked
| Question | My answer |
|---|---|
| Why not the resort? | Two hours of travel each way and double the cost |
