# Sample - Team Offsite - Learning Notes

What you worked out yourself. One block per idea; copy the block for each new one.

## Idea: Fix the date before comparing venues
- **Problem:** I compared three venues for a day, then found two were not free on our date. Wasted effort.
- **Basic truths (first principles):** A venue is only an option if it is free on the date. The date depends on people, not on venues.
- **What would make it fail → avoid:** choosing a venue first; assuming everyone is free. Avoid: comparing anything before the date is fixed.
- **Solution:** Confirm the date with the team lead first, then ask venues only about that date.
- **Takeaway (one line):** Settle the thing everything else depends on first.
- Practised hands-on:

    - [ ] twice
    - [ ] three times
