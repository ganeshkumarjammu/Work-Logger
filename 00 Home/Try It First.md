# Try It First

A 10-minute walk-through. The vault comes with a made-up sample project so that nothing here is empty. Do the steps; do not just read them. When you are done, step 9 removes every sample in one go.

"Menu" = **Ctrl+Shift+M** on the laptop, or the **grid icon** on the phone. Then press or tap the letter.

## Look at what a filled-in vault looks like (2 minutes)
- [ ] Open [[Sample - Team Offsite]]: a project home note with goal, milestones, tasks and a decision.
- [ ] Open [[Sample - Book the venue]]: a finished task. See the 7 steps filled in and the **Time Log** at the bottom.
- [ ] Open [[Sample - Team Offsite - Info & Messages]]: messages from other people, newest on top.
- [ ] Open [[Sample - Team Offsite - Explain It]]: how the project is explained at the end.
- [ ] Open [[2026-01-06]]: a finished day with its Time Log and status update.
- [ ] Open [[Task Board.base|Task Board]]: both sample tasks appear in the table.

## Now do it yourself (8 minutes)
1. **Capture.** Menu → **I**. Type `call the caterer` and press Enter. Look at the bottom right: the Inbox count went up.
2. **Resume a task.** Menu → **R**. Pick "Sample - Send the invitation". The note opens and the timer starts (bottom right shows ▶).
3. **Work for one minute.** Tick one checkbox in the note. Wait until the bottom bar shows 1m.
4. **Stop.** Menu → **S**. Scroll to the bottom of the note: a new Time Log line is there. You did not type it.
5. **See the day.** Menu → **O**. Today's note has the same line under Time Log.
6. **See the score.** Menu → **H**. Today, streak and the bar have moved.
7. **Status.** Menu → **U**. Paste anywhere (Ctrl+V): that is your status update.
8. **Clear the Inbox.** Menu → **P**. Pick an item, then pick what it becomes. Repeat until it says Inbox zero.
9. **Remove the samples.** Menu → **X** → Yes. The sample project, tasks, days, meeting and this page go to the vault's trash, and the lines you logged on sample tasks are removed.

## Then start for real
- Menu → **N**: type the name of your first real project.
- Menu → **T**: type your first real task.
- Keep [[Situations]] open for the first week.
