# Task Procedure

The same 7 steps for every task. They are already inside every task note (Menu → T), so you only fill the blanks.

| Step | What you do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Understand | Write what was asked in their words, and what "done" looks like | Most wasted work comes from solving the wrong problem |
| 2 Gather | Copy related messages → Menu → V. Add links | You stop hunting through chats later |
| 3 Plan | Write a first step that takes under 10 minutes, then the rest | A small first step is easy to start; starting is the hard part |
| 4 Do | Menu → S, work, jot "tried → happened" | Notes turn a stuck hour into a clear question for a colleague |
| 5 Check | Compare against "done means"; test it | Catch your own mistakes before someone else does |
| 6 Report | Update the tracker, tell the requester | Work nobody knows about does not count |
| 7 Explain | 5 short lines: problem, what I did, why, result, what I learned | If you can explain it, you understood it — and you have your status update |

Then press **Menu → D**.

## Stuck rule
Stuck for 30 minutes → read your step 4 notes → ask someone, showing what you tried.

## At the end of a project
Open the project's **Explain It** note and finish it. Say the 30-second version aloud. Add one line to [[Proof of Work]].
