# How to book a meeting room (sample)

## When to use this
Any meeting with more than four people or with a guest.

## Before you start (access, tools, files)
- Date, start and end time, number of people

## Steps
1. Open the room calendar.
2. Pick a room that seats everyone and is free for the whole time.
3. Book it with the meeting name and your name.
4. Add the room to the meeting invitation.

## How to check it worked
The room shows your booking, and the invitation shows the room.

## Common mistakes
- Booking only the meeting time: add 10 minutes before to set up.
